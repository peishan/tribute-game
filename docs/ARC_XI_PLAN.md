# ARC XI — THE VANISHING OF LADY VEYNE (chapters 263–286)

Author's design document (saved as sent; the message was cut off at the "Art assets required" table, so that section is missing).

**Theme:** Not everyone who disappears wishes to be found. Not everyone who stays behind has been forgotten.
**Focus:** Jade Gold, Devon Chadstone, Lady Mirelle Veyne. Secondary: Delilah Dee, Roc Chadwick, Crown Prince Rheon Altan.
**Setting:** Altan → Dragonvale → Veyne Estate → Dragonvale's northern territories.
**Tone:** Noble intrigue, investigation, personal growth, supernatural suspense, friendship, occasional comedy. Arc X was Sera's home and inheritance; Arc XI returns to Jade's investigations, with Devon more prominent. The mystery begins ordinarily: an elderly noblewoman has disappeared and someone is eager to declare her dead.
**Adaptation note:** text-based story modals for most chapters; illustrated splashes for the arc, significant discoveries and bosses.

## Chapters
- **263 The Prince Who Wouldn't Stay Put.** Sera stays in Altan; Rheon (Crown Prince, "not king") travels as Altan's representative; succession unresolved. Rheon recruitable; Sera to reserve; Companion Assignment system; companions may stay at regional hubs without losing recruitment; Rheon's first abilities. Objective: complete the party transition, prepare for Dragonvale. *Rheon is not renouncing the throne yet.*
- **264 A Falcon Beyond Altan.** Rheon's first journey. Rin investigates frontier movements separately; Eira takes temporary leave (not permanent). Rheon's first field-training objective; Rin temporarily unavailable; Eira stays recruitable; Regional Companion Assignments. *Rin and Eira remain in Jade's wider network.*
- **265 The Road to Dragonvale.** Devon's message: a gathering of the noble houses; Lady Mirelle Veyne has not been seen. Route, road encounters, Rheon combat tutorial, optional camp dialogue with Devon.
- **266 An Empty Chair.** Mirelle's seat is empty; she vanished weeks ago; her household claims she died travelling north, no body; a petition to recognise her death. Dragonvale Noble Court, Mirelle investigation, House Veyne dossier, first dialogue choices. Key reveal: someone is attempting to have Mirelle declared dead.
- **267 The Lady Who Advised Princes.** Devon tells Jade about Mirelle. Dossier, relationship scene, interview former household staff, lore entry House Veyne. Objective: three accounts.
- **268 The Woman in the Library.** Delilah studying military law and administration; Roc notices. Delilah's Administrative Studies, Dragonvale Academy, Delilah's questline. *Delilah is a student, not yet an adviser.*
- **269 Words Long Overdue.** Delilah apologises; Jade accepts but trust takes time. Reconciliation Stage I. *Fixed story development, not a forgiveness route.*
- **270 The Petition of Death.** Fast filing, missing witnesses, a distant relative gets temporary control. Document examination, evidence comparison, Legal Records Investigation, first contradiction. Devon requests suspension.
- **271 A Student's Observation.** Delilah notices shipments to House Veyne's northern estate after Mirelle's supposed death; redirected to an abandoned military station. Support investigation bonus, northern supply route, trace shipments.
- **272 The Estate Without Its Lady.** Veyne Estate; frightened servants; dismissed officials. Estate location, servant interviews, hidden documents, Mirelle's private study.
- **273 The Locked Study.** Replaced lock; correspondence about northern land grants; a page removed from the ledger. Environmental puzzle, evidence reconstruction, Missing Ledger Page.
- **274 The Prince and the Ranger.** Rheon in the northern countryside treats it like a siege. Rheon field objective, tactical combat, Falcon Vanguard: Interception.
- **275 The Road That Changed Names.** Renamed roads; recent movement; the station may not be abandoned. Route exploration, tracking, hidden path.
- **276 The Station Beneath the Pines.** Mercenaries, stored supplies and documents. Dungeon, mini-boss Northern Station Captain, recover records.
- **277 The Name Beneath the Seal.** Fraudulent inheritance claims; Mirelle requested an independent audit. Noble Conspiracy stage, return to Dragonvale, suspect dossiers.
- **278 A Witness Who Should Be Dead.** A forged signature; Mirelle was moved north under guard. Interrogation, rescue objective.
- **279 Beyond the Northern Gate.** Northern valley; medicinal supplies suggest a captive. Stealth or tactical entry, Rheon defensive challenge.
- **280 The Lady Who Refused to Die.** Mirelle found alive ("I shall remember that suggestion the next time I am abducted."). Rescue, protected NPC, escape.
- **281 The Price of Silence.** The conspiracy spans officials; a conspirator destroys evidence. Timed evidence recovery.
- **282 Judgment at Dragonvale.** The conspiracy exposed lawfully; boss: The Veyne Conspirator's Champion. *The mastermind is held accountable through Dragonvale's legal institutions, not killed in combat.*
- **283 The Lady Returns.** Escort Mirelle home; House Veyne reputation; Mirelle as a recurring advisory NPC.
- **284 A Different Kind of Noblewoman.** Delilah meets Mirelle; Studies Stage II; Mirelle Mentorship. *No promotion or proposal yet.*
- **285 Princes Without Thrones.** Rheon's first companion quest; Falcon Vanguard: Royal Guard Formation. *No abdication or succession decision yet.*
- **286 The Road Beyond Dragonvale.** Mirelle's advice to Devon; the forgotten alliance resurfaces elsewhere; next arc teaser; Rheon remains recruitable.

## Rheon (author's initial design)
Falcon Vanguard, defensive warrior / battlefield commander, affinity Falcon Warfire, royal spear or sword. Abilities: Falcon Strike and Vanguard Guard (recruitment), Falcon Interception (ch274: counters enemies targeting vulnerable allies), Royal Guard Formation (ch285: temporary party defence), White Warfire (future). Not stronger than Sera in every category; Sera is offence, Rheon protection and leadership.

## Delilah (long-term)
Not a combat character. Student (personal quest) → Apprentice (minor investigation help) → Administrator (supply and travel bonuses) → Recognised Official (diplomatic/administrative benefits) → Lady Delilah Dee (regional support) → Roc's Wife (a narrative milestone, not a gameplay reward).
