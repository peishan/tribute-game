- All 25 chapters: pages, four chained spots each (`hz362a`...`hz386d`), 25 missions, lore, flags. New place **The Imperial Archives** (ch376; the Dragonvale North map's list); everything else at the Dragonvale Noble Court.
- **Crown Candidates** Archive tab (from ch362): the court's four candidates and the four women Jade and Aster met, what each wants and what Devon judged, with lines unlocking as clues are found; **House Xuanji** and the twin record appear once ch381 and ch384 are read.
- **Lady Yulan Fen: Dragonvale Ally** (author's design, from ch386; flag `arc15_complete`): not a party member and no combat. Panel in the Case tab with the fate line (she did not become Crown Princess; she later sat the imperial examinations, placed first as Zhuangyuan and entered government service as a scholar). **Scholar's Insight:** research projects take about 10% less time (a day saved every few projects), and every third project turns up a lead (+1 Seal Dust, a stand-in for "Research Lead Found", which would reveal an extra clue or destination: not built). **Eight optional quests** at the Dragonvale Academy (a missing manuscript, two records that disagree, an inscription, a genealogy, an escort, an unknown artifact, a forged charter, two accounts of one war): scholarly, not political. Much-later envoy career is not built. Portrait: the author's (a black court hat, purple robes).
- **Aster: Dragonvale Ally** (built from the author's note "Aster's portrait is up next, to build him as an ally"; his design was not given, so this is a minimal first pass): a Case-tab panel and **Royal Counsel** (one word of advice every 4 days: XP and a little court renown). He is not a party member and does not fight. Portrait: the author's (green dragon robes, a gold crown). **Please send his ally design if you want more** (a combat guest, a political favour, or something else).
- The gap: the brief's "Aster observes the royal family at Delilah and Roc's gathering" opening hook is not in chapter 361, and the first Arc XV page is "After Arc XIV, Aster receives formal candidate dossiers".

## Mismatches and open questions
1. **ch366 vs ch365:** the court presents Wen Qingyu, Lin Yao, Elin of Solen and Shen Huizhen, but ch366 on show four different women (Seraphine Lin, Yulan Fen, Elise Valen, Meirun Zhao). The later chapters follow the second four, with Lin Rui (the General's niece) as the "Lady Seraphine Lin" military candidate. Both lists are in the Crown Candidates tab and flagged as the court's list and the women Jade met.
2. **The candidates' names:** the scholar is "Yulan Fen" and the page also says "Shen Yulan Fen" (and her father "Minister Yulan Fen"); ch365 has a "Lady Shen Huizhen" too. The border lady is never named.
3. **ch385 repeats ch375** almost word for word (the terrace conversation), then adds the decision; kept as written.
4. **ch386:** the page puts "I accept this position" on a figure in lilac robes with wisteria hairpieces (the scholar's daughter's look) while the text says the border lady. Built as the border lady accepting, per the story text. Check the art.
5. **ch383:** Aster says "Both myself and Devon are to be recorded as the Queen's sons: twins", but the twins are Roc and Devon (Aster is the younger half-brother, per ch384). The game text says the twins are recorded.
6. **ch384:** Roc is "former prince, currently a noble and maritime officer after returning from exile" while the cover calls Aster "Crown Prince (Half-Brother)" and Devon "Twin Brother"; Devon is "current prince". Kept as written.
7. **Zhuangyuan:** the fate line spells the title "Zhuangyuan" (Zhuàngyuán); the Chinese characters are not in the game text.
8. **Names:** Xuanji (玄玑) is the royal house; Roc 宗鹏, Devon 宗彦, Aster 宗曜; King Cassian 景承; Queen Estelle Veyra 卫星澜. Earlier game text calls Roc "Roc Chadstone" and "Chadwick" in places.
