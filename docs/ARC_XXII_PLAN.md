# ARC XXII — THE HOUSE THAT REMAINS (chapters 550-580; title from the author, cover to come)
Theme: blood may define a house, but choices decide what it becomes. **Primary focus:** Roc, Delilah, Devon, Aster. **Secondary:** Jade, Fen, Elise, Tarin. **Canon travelling party:** Jade, Devon, Sky, Levi, Sally (no guest listed). Tone: political intrigue, family drama, military investigation, reflection, quiet romance. **Fifteen Evils: no new resolution; the tracker stays 7 / 15 resolved, 8 remaining.** Starts after Arc XXI (ch549).
Canon this arc rests on: the twin reveal has happened (ch381-384: King Cassian Chadstone and Queen Estelle Veyra; Roc and Devon twins, Aster their younger half-brother); Elise and Aster are married (515: alliance, separate rooms, each free to love: Elise Tarin, Aster Fen); Fen is a scholar and is with Aster; Elise is secretly meeting Tarin; Aster's ascension depends on circumstances, not a fixed age (and in this arc the circumstance is his father's death at 576).
Main conflict: someone is deliberately using the twin revelation to destabilise House Xuanji, giving each faction a different rumour (Devon will challenge Aster; Roc has the stronger claim; Jade is positioning Devon with her international influence; Aster fears both brothers; the frontier marriage proves Aster's claim was weak). None of the brothers believes it. Mystery: who benefits? Meanwhile the king's illness makes succession gossip dangerous. The conspiracy is human (greed, ambition, fear), not an Evil.
