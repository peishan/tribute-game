# ARC XXIV: THE LIVES WE CHOOSE (planned; subject to change until the chapters are final)
Relationship-and-home arc. No Evil resolved (tracker stays 8 / 15), no major Black Pearl material. Source: the author's roadmap (docs/ROADMAP_XXIII_TO_ENDGAME.md) and the notes below. Nothing is built.

## Strands
1. **Sky and Jenika.** Sky's Valen resonance is the key to breaking Jenika's curse; the cure is an intimacy cure and a **one-time event** (not repeatable, no route or spend-time option; handled tastefully, off-page). Jenika survives; later she learns she is pregnant. The Sky/Jenika material stays untouched in Arc XXIII.
2. **Elise's departure and pretended death** (the author's design, replaces the earlier "line of narration" idea):
   - Aster privately releases Elise from the political marriage (the frontier is stable); Elise chooses Tarin. If a pregnancy is used it speeds her departure but does not cause the choice.
   - Elise leaves with Tarin for the secluded life. Her **death from illness is faked**, and the kingdom **mourns her for 45 days**.
   - **Fen stands in for her:** Fen wears a wig and poses as Elise (the two are the same height, so it is feasible). Fen is already in and out of court as a royal scholar, so nobody notices anything; Elise\'s illness explains a reduced, veiled public presence before the "death".
3. **Aster and Fen\'s unannounced wedding.** Held privately, like his first wedding to Elise, which was not official but held at the frontier. Fen becomes his wife in secret.
4. **The announcement.** After about **90 to 100 days** Aster announces Fen as his queen. This is in line with Dragonvale law: he is young and the kingdom needs a queen. Aster\'s reform ending the royal polygamous marriage structure (see the roadmap) follows his marriage to Fen.

## Ends of the arc
Sky/Jenika: family begins. Aster/Fen: wed, then announced. Elise/Tarin: free and hidden. Evils 8 / 15.

## Proposed light mechanics (from the author; to build when the chapters arrive, not a heavy systems arc)
XXIV is about unlocking, not new combat: disguise and covert-identity quests, Elise and Tarin's hidden-network quests, and future reform hooks.
1. **Elise and Tarin: Hidden Frontier Network.** They were already unlocked earlier (Elise Border Ally, Tarin Border Trail panels), so this *upgrades* their function. **Unlocked after the end of the arc, when Elise and Tarin leave.** They are no longer court-facing: they are useful because they sit outside official structures. Quest types: discreet frontier quests, escort jobs, intelligence gathering, refugee help, identity-protection missions. A different quest identity from Roc, Delilah, Aster and Jenika.
2. **Disguise missions.** Aster\'s plan introduces a reusable stealth/disguise quest type: limited appearances, avoiding recognition, choosing routes, carrying forged or altered identities.
3. **Court Suspicion Meter.** Only for this arc, simple, not a permanent major system (it must not become a social sim). Mishandled appearances or inconsistencies raise it. Keep it separate from the existing masks suspicion in repute.js.
4. **Hidden Identity flag.** Elise is publicly dead, privately alive. Later quests can branch on whether the player protects the secret.
5. **Pavilion support access.** Jenika\'s role can unlock expanded healing/support services or medical side quests at the Royal Healing Pavilion (unlocked ch46), without turning her into a shopkeeper NPC.
6. **Dragonvale Reform Prelude.** Once Fen is queen, start tracking House Xuanji reforms as a future side-quest category (see the roadmap\'s late-game reforms).
