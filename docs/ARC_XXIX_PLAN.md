# ARC XXIX: THE STONE THAT REMEMBERS (the Weeping Stone; title from the teaser card on ch735)
Chapters 736-738 are built so far: Sky's memories have recovered (gradually, not suddenly), the party leaves Altan for **Lingering Vale** by the road Sky remembers (he corrects an old map: the winter landslide closed one pass), and he speaks openly about **Master Yale**, who raised him.

## What the pages settle
- Sky remembers growing up in the Vale, Yale raising and training him, his healer education, Yvette in his later childhood and leaving the Vale. That Yvette brought him as an infant is something he learned later, not a memory. "There are things I forgot, and there are things I simply never knew."
- Yale is strict, teaches diagnosis before treatment, and cares more than he admits. His being Sky's father is **not** said on any page and is not built.
- A strange disturbance has appeared in Lingering Vale. The Weeping Stone is the Evil to come (13 / 15); it is not yet registered as active.
