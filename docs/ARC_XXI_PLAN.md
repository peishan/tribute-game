The two hidden "???" slots (14 and 15) are used for the Arc XX and Arc XXI Evils; the named placeholders are kept for future arcs. Progress: 5 / 15 before Arc XX, 6 / 15 after Arc XX, 7 / 15 after Arc XXI (8 remaining).
