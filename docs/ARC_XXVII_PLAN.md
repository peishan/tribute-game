## The fight (ch707, scripts/skyeater.js)
Four phases as the page labels them: **Hunger** (it drains the party's MP each round; Jade's Hold Formation halves it), **Falling Shadow** (a tell, then a wave; Levi's Mark Openings turns it aside, Levi alive halves it), **Devour the Light** (three ward nodes, one lost per round; Sky stabilises, Devon reinforces the barriers, Sally distracts the shadows) and **Silver Crown** (its aerial defences take 30% damage until Rheon breaks them; Rheon joins the fight here even if he was not one of the five). Companions and auto-battle use the actions themselves. A standing replay is at Altan Capital. Numbers are first-pass.
