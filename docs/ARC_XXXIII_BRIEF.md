# ARC XXXIII — SISTERS RISE: the author's brief (chapters 831-850; provisional until the pages are drawn)
Evil count 15/15. Focus: Jade, Adrian; secondary Devon, Sky, Dima, Xima. Theme: history remembers the winner more easily than the truth. Arc question: what actually happened between Dima and Xima?

**Purpose:** reconstruct the real Dima/Xima history from the deeper records opened in Arc XXXII. Reveal that the False Ancestor was not Xima, and why later generations confused them; Xima deliberately created exactly fifteen corrupted anchors (a completed cycle) inside a pre-existing system; Xima is responsible but not a simple evil sister; Dima also made serious mistakes; a third influence manipulated or intensified the conflict; end with the first undeniable proof that someone benefited from keeping Lunareth divided. **The manipulator is not identified yet (Arc XXXIV).**

**Part I, the history beneath history:** 831 The Sealed Record (preserved memory structures: writing, images, voices, ritual patterns; ancient does not mean accurate); 832 Two Sisters (working together on the restoration system: Dima healing, preservation, stabilisation; Xima containment, boundaries, controlled separation; the system needed both); 833 Before the Curse (Lunareth already unstable; the system restored land, preserved life, regulated passages, kept regions from collapsing into each other); 834 The First Disagreement (how much should stay connected: sealing suffocates vs connection destroys); 835 The Keeper Records (Keepers maintained records, monitored passages, advised the sisters; neutral language turns accusatory: Dima reckless, Xima dangerous: someone shaped the narrative).
**Part II, the False Ancestor:** 836 The Name That Should Not Be There (it existed during the conflict; not another name for Xima: "someone combined two different stories"); 837 What the False Ancestor Was (an ancient constructed or altered being associated with the old system; not one of the Fifteen, not Xima, not Dima; a visible symbol of catastrophe); 838 Why History Confused Them (records collapsed together over centuries); 839 Jade's Correction (the Imperial Guard archive adds "Identity previously assumed. Evidence now contradicts it."; she will not replace one certainty with another).
**Part III, why fifteen:** 840 Fifteen Positions (arranged as a completed lunar cycle; chosen, not accidental); 841 The Completed Cycle (one anchor destabilises a region, several block the network, fifteen let the corrupted cycle sustain itself: the network alive enough to function imperfectly, never healthy enough to restore itself); 842 What the Evils Were (system positions, functions or anchors turned into autonomous entities; centuries of change explain their differences); 843 Why Xima Did It (she believed collapse was coming and chose division and corruption to force isolated survivable regions, knowingly sacrificing movement, connection, continuity and lives; Jade understands the logic without excusing it); 844 Dima's Mistake (aggressive restoration pushed damaged structures past what they could bear: "Dima = pure good / Xima = pure evil" breaks); 845 The Sisters Break (they accuse each other of choosing control over life, and ideals over survival; outside manipulation becomes easier).
**Part IV, someone else was there:** 846 The Missing Voice (pauses, answers to unheard statements, instructions never recorded; matching gaps in Keeper records); 847 The Compass and the Star (the same symbolic pattern as earlier incidents: compass anomalies, star imagery, altered directions); 848 The Advice That Divided Them (each sister was shown true but incomplete evidence: selective truth); 849 The Beneficiary (after the division movement was harder, regions isolated, Keepers dependent on filtered information, history fragmented, routes easier to control indirectly: someone gained from nobody seeing the whole system); 850 Sisters Rise (both sisters realise too late that their conflict may have been manipulated; the memory cuts out before the presence is revealed; Jade: "Who needed them to believe the other one would?"; the path to the deeper Dima/Xima regions is unstable and the surviving corruption must be purified, not destroyed). **NEXT: ARC XXXIV, PURIFICATION.**

**Results:** Dima no longer blameless; Xima responsible for the Fifteen but not one-note; the Fifteen were pre-existing positions/anchors, intentionally numbered, fifteen = a completed cycle; the False Ancestor confirmed not Xima and the official record corrected; the Keepers were ancient maintainers whose records were selectively altered; a third influence used selective truth and benefited from division; identity withheld. Jade stays an investigator who will not swap a myth for an equally unsupported one; Adrian becomes central to reconstructing history.
