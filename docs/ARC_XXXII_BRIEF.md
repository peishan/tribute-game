# ARC XXXII — RETURNING PEARL: the author's brief (chapters 811-830)
Evil count 15/15 complete. Focus: Devon, Jade; secondary Adrian, Sky, Aster, Fen. Theme: carrying something does not mean possessing it. Arc question: why did the Pearl choose Devon's line, and why is the network calling for it now?

**Must explain:** what the restoration network was built to do; why it woke during the later arcs; why destroying all fifteen let it respond fully; why Devon's ancestors carried the Pearl; why it could not simply have been returned earlier; why Devon is a legitimate bearer/custodian; how the Pearl works as catalyst and stabilising anchor; how the network becomes self-sustaining. **Must not answer:** who the Pearl's ultimate true owner is. Establish only that Devon was never carrying it by mistake.

**Part I, the network calls:** 811 The Call ("Is it calling you?" "No." ... "Something is calling it."); 812 Fifteen Doors Open (15/15 was the condition required to access the system); 813 The Pearl Chamber (the late-game version completely supersedes the earlier placeholder encounter); 814 Bearer (not owner, king, master or chosen one: Bearer, then Custodian); 815 Before House Xuanji (Fen and Adrian: the Pearl predates the dynasty and House Xuanji; Black Dragon powers come from bloodline, the Pearl is something else).
**Part II, the people who carried it:** 816 The First Custodians (the network destabilised; the Pearl was carried away so corruption could not spread through it; the duty became associated with House Xuanji's ancestors, not necessarily the first custodians); 817 Why a Bloodline? (continuity, resonance, repeated custodial contact, compatible constitutions; Devon is one of the strongest surviving matches); 818 Roc and Devon (the Pearl reacts to Roc differently, weaker and less stable: it is not just "a Chadstone prince"; Roc is irritated for about five minutes); 819 The Drowned Crown (the earlier restoration of authority to Devon was the network recognising a legitimate bearer, not naming an owner); 820 Why It Could Not Return (no safe place: fragmented, corrupted, blocked by the Fifteen; duty: carry it until the network can safely call).
**Part III, what the Pearl does:** 821 The Restoration Network (a map of living channels, nodes, conduits, former Evil sites; built to preserve and restore damaged life and environments: not resurrection, not immortality); 822 The Limit (can stabilise, repair, restore function, preserve near failure, assist ecosystems and structures; cannot restore the truly dead; ties Bone River, Mirror Queen, the Pearl saving Sky, Mirelle); 823 The Pearl as Catalyst (catalyst, key, stabiliser, reference point; the network is now healthy enough to use it); 824 Not Forever ("It needs the Pearl to remember how to stand. Not necessarily to keep standing forever."); 825 The First Restoration (Devon places the Pearl in the interface without surrendering ownership or completing a prophecy; channels stabilise, some land recovers, leaks stop; healing takes time).
**Part IV, the custodian who remains:** 826 Devon Without the Pearl (his dragon powers are unaffected; a faint resonance remains); 827 The Living Custodian (senses major disturbances, recognises Pearl activity, accesses certain structures, acts as the human intermediary); 828 The Question of the True Owner ("the one to whom the Pearl returns": rightful owner, origin, source, final bearer or one who receives it at the end of a cycle; the texts contradict; no answer; Jade decides not every question must be forced into an answer); 829 The Pearl Returns to Devon (the network releases it, keeps a resonance imprint and keeps functioning); 830 The Door Beneath the Network (sealed records older than most accounts of Dima and Xima: two female figures, fifteen positions, deliberate alteration, an entity later misidentified, and a section altered by someone other than either sister: another influence; Jade: "Who taught us to ask the question that way?").

**Results:** Evils remain 15/15 (their removal was the condition for the network to communicate); network built to preserve and restore damaged life, environments and magical structures, cannot resurrect, was blocked by the Fifteen, begins large-scale restoration and becoming self-sustaining, keeps a resonance imprint; the Pearl predates Dragonvale and House Xuanji, saved Sky because preservation is part of its nature, returns temporarily to stabilise the network, is later released to Devon, may be removable permanently once the network is self-sustaining, true owner unknown. Devon: legitimate bearer and custodian, dragon abilities independent of the Pearl, a living custodian even when apart from it. House Xuanji: custodians, not necessarily owners. Jade: interrogating historical assumptions. Adrian and Fen: research partners (a pattern for the later Crimson Tide crossover with Renn, Erynn and Mimi). Sky: ties the Pearl and network to earlier restoration phenomena. Endgame reveal: oldest records disagree with accepted Dima/Xima history and a third influence altered them; the manipulator is not identified. Transition: from "what was the Pearl built to do?" to "what actually happened between Dima and Xima?"
