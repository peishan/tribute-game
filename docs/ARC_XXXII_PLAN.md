# ARC XXXII: RETURNING PEARL (chapters 811-829 built; 830 and the cover not sent)
Opens right after 15 / 15. Per ENDGAME_PLAN this arc is the Pearl Chamber rebuild: Devon's Black Pearl is called home by the awakened network.

## What the pages show
- **811 The Call:** the Pearl pulls against Devon's chest, like a direction inside his body; stronger toward Dragonvale. "Something is calling it."
- **812 Fifteen Doors Open:** Adrian's reports: all fifteen former sites changed (reopened sealed passages, restorative energy, old markings, links to structures beneath neighbouring regions). Their removal released fifteen blocked points in a much larger system: "15/15 was not the end of the system. It was the condition required to access it."
- **813 The Pearl Chamber:** back in Dragonvale the old Pearl Chamber / Dragon Sanctuary reacts before Devon enters; doors open, symbols glow; the Pearl and the chamber recognise each other.
- **814 Bearer:** an inscription calls Devon neither owner, king, master nor chosen one but **Bearer**, then **Custodian**. The system expected someone to carry the Pearl.
