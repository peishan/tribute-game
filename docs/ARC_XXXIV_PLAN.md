# ARC XXXIV: PURIFICATION (chapters 851-854 built from pages; no brief or cover yet)
Opens right after Sisters Rise (850). The Arc XXXIV brief has not been sent; this records what the pages show, and the drawn pages win over any later brief draft.

- **851 What Comes After Truth:** knowing Dima and Xima were manipulated fixes nothing; the restoration network is awake but unstable (some regions recovering, others flooded with restorative energy, old barriers weakening faster than surrounding lands can bear). "We stop asking who broke it and start asking how to repair it."
- **852 The Purification Map:** the network cannot be reactivated globally and needs staged purification, node by node, in order: **Dragonvale, Tribute, the Older Seal Regions, the Altan-linked Routes, then Dima/Xima Territory (the southeastern routes)**. Dragonvale is first. This is likely the arc's spine.
- **853 Return to Dragonvale:** ordinary life continued. Aster rules; **Fen** (Queen and Royal Scholar) and **Jenika** are visibly pregnant (a page joke: one of the travelling party fails to understand it); **Roc and Delilah** have newborn twins.
- **854 Two Babies and No Sleep:** an interlude: the twins were born early (as twin pregnancies are), Delilah recovers, Liora is the older sister. "Fighting Evils was easier."
