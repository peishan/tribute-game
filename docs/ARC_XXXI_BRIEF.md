# ARC XXXI — THE MIRROR QUEEN: the author's brief (received after the chapters were built)
Chapters 786-810. Evil count 14/15 -> 15/15. Focus: Jade, Devon; secondary Mirelle, Rheon, Sky, Adrian. Theme: what is restored is not always what should return. Arc question: if you could become the person you once were, should you?

**Purpose:** the final Fifteen-Evil hunt; bring Mirelle's old Mirror restoration back into relevance; the Mirror Queen corrupted an ancient restoration structure and is an entity that learned to preserve, duplicate and restore but no longer understands restoration versus replacement (not "an evil woman in a mirror"); the Fifteen were deliberately connected to older systems; 15/15 triggers the deeper system so the Black Pearl responds; transition to Arc XXXII.

Or replace?"; 800 Devon refuses a life without his burdens; 801 the true chamber; 802 evidence Xima deliberately altered the system (fifteen was intentional); 803 the fifteenth seat ("And what wakes when that happens?"); 804 the Queen copies the party; 805 Jade's copy ("You are not me anymore"); 806 the true core: a mirror-array around a humanoid figure of layered reflections; 807 boss (Reflected Party, Restoration Loop, Perfect Reflection, Break the Pattern); 808 Fifteen (sites react synchronously; the Fifteen were occupying the system); 809 the Pearl answers ("The bearer carries the Pearl until the network calls it home"); 810 Returning Pearl ("Fifteen was never the ending." / "It was the lock.").

**Results:** Evils 15/15; Mirror Queen = corrupted restoration entity; restoration vs resurrection established; Mirelle's restoration was genuine; Jade stays the chooser; Devon the active bearer; Sky confirms the limit (damaged life can be restored, true death cannot be reversed); evidence implicates Xima without her motive; the Pearl remains with Devon as key/catalyst and ownership stays unresolved.
