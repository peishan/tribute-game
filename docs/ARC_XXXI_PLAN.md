Ch785 ends Arc XXX with the teaser card **"15th Evil: Mirror Queen. Next: Arc XXXI, The Mirror Queen."** It states that **fourteen had fallen and one remained**, so the Sunless Child is marked resolved by ch784. **Chapters 782-784 have not been sent** (they must show its fall): its fight, the choice at the passage structure and its aftermath are not built.

## What the pages show
- **785-786:** mirrors misbehave across Lunareth (reflections move first, show younger, older, injured or dead selves, rooms that do not exist); the last marker lights; Jade understands defeating the Fifteen might only open the door. Then people vanish and their reflection remains.
- **787, 789:** Mirelle (pregnant, in Altan) feels her old Mirror reacting; Rheon wants her safe; she will assist the investigation without joining every dangerous encounter. In 789 she explains the Mirror repaired her body, injuries and her ability to have a child: it was part of a network of ancient restoration structures, perhaps not made for people like her.
- **788:** an affected mirror compares you against other possible versions of yourself (timelines and possibilities). Jade sees herself younger, as the Imperial Guard, crowned, dead, alone and beside Devon, and covers it.
- **790-792:** the **first replacement**: a missing person returns but is a reconstruction built from what the Mirror remembered (incomplete spiritual pattern); the original is alive but trapped in the mirror; the replacement believes she is the original. Jade refuses to treat either as expendable. Adrian and Sky argue what counts as a copy; Jade asks what is owed to a being that can think, feel and suffer. The seed returns when the Gold Child's choice (preserve, open, restrict, allow to continue) comes.
- **793-796:** Levi finds the incidents along the old **Mirror Roads** (fourteen nodes quiet, the fifteenth feeding). Mirelle's Veyne records name the **Queen Behind the Glass**, a title from before the dynasties, perhaps the origin of the network. The ancient site's four functions (preserve bodies, rebuild tissue, stabilise life, record patterns) distorted into a system that could replicate. The system can restore damaged life but **not someone truly dead**; where the Queen refused that limit she made copies.
- **797-800:** the Queen speaks through every reflective surface: "patterns are the truth beneath life", offering to reproduce the dead, restore the aged, replace what grief lost; "she has confused continuity with perfection." She tempts **Mirelle** (a younger self before widowhood; refused: "that is the real me"), **Jade** ("restore, or replace?": the Queen sees no difference) and **Devon** (a life without House Xuanji or the Pearl; refused). Sky, Levi and Sally are not tempted on the pages so far.
