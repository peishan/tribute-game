# ARC XXX: THE SUNLESS CHILD (chapters 761-781 built; cover in: "When light is devoured, choice becomes the last sun.")
**The next arc brings the story back to Tribute** (the author): ch763 returns the party to Tribute, to Adrian's library.

## What the pages show
- **761 Light That Does Not Return:** reports from the northern marches, the eastern coast and the central cities of places where the sun is visible but its light does not arrive: lamps will not burn, shadows misbehave. Not an eclipse; Jade says it bears the mark of another Evil. Something is devouring the light.
- **762 The Old Gold Record:** Adrian, from the Gold estates, finds an old record (older than many versions of the Gold Child prophecy) that reads as a description, not a metaphor: *"The child of Gold who walks beneath a sun that cannot reach her."* Jade is tired of riddles about her bloodline but asks for the full copy.
- **763 Not the First Version:** back in Tribute, Adrian lays out the versions of the prophecy (a child who opens the world, seals it, restores what was divided, destroys the old order, belongs to Dima, belongs to neither sister). None can be confirmed as the original: copied, edited, translated, used by many people. The prophecy has a history, and the party will trace where each version came from.

## Evils
**The Sunless Child** is registered as the next Evil (active from 761, unresolved: 13 / 15). No place or nature is shown yet, so none is guessed.

## Chapters 764-781 (built)
- **764-765:** Hugh explains how Tribute has read the Gold Child; Adrian's doubt: if it meant any Gold descendant there would have been earlier candidates, so the prophecy is **both a bloodline prophecy and a world-state prophecy** that only activates as the Fifteen fall and the seals are undone.
- **766-770:** the first sunless village (twilight at midday, plants that do not turn to the sun, vanishing shadows); a shadowless child whom villagers call the Sunless Child (**not literally him**); the five conflicting legends and Jade's rule (event, legend, doctrine, an excuse to hurt someone); Levi finds the affected areas follow the ancient seal-and-energy routes (a deliberate manipulation of an existing system); Sky separates the child from the corruption as at the Weeping Stone: the child was only a door.
- **771-773:** a dormant inscription names Jade the **"Gold-blooded Chooser"** (not queen, not vessel, not sacrifice); scholars and officials try to protect, confine, shrine or ritualise her, and she refuses; Devon's private question: what if it is real? Jade rejects other people's version of it, not the truth.
- **774-775:** the **Sunless Archive** (older than the current kingdoms) calls the Gold Child "the one who may determine passage" and lists six passage categories (open, conditional, shared, restricted, sealed, unclassified, details missing). "Sunless Child" never meant a human child: it named an ancient entity **born beneath a dead sun**; the name survived while the meaning was lost. **The Fourteenth Evil is identified.**
- **776-778:** it feeds through the network and amplifies itself; Jade sees broken fragments (Dima, Xima, sealed roads, divided regions, a golden figure at branching paths); the structure shows **five incompatible possible structures** (open everything, seal everything, divide access, destroy the structure, one unfinished): possibilities, not predictions.
- **779:** Adrian: the prophecy is a **decision framework mistaken for a prediction**, kept by scribes branch by branch; it asks what Jade will do. **780-781:** the Sunless Child manifests (partly physical, partly an absence; fused with a solar restoration conduit) and shows Jade possible futures to make her fear choosing wrong.
- **No fight is shown yet:** 781 ends before the battle. The count stays 13 / 15.
