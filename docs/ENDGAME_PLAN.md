# ENDGAME PLAN: LOCKED BY THE AUTHOR (chapters not built yet)
Why this exists: the Black Pearl threads (docs/BLACK_PEARL_AND_PROPHECY.md) change the shape of the finale. The old plan was **15/15 Evils, then the Dima/Xima purification, then postgame**. The proposal below adds one layer: the restoration network and the Pearl come back before the sisters do, and the last revelation comes after the sisters, not with them.
Legend: **LOCKED** = already canon in the built chapters (chapter given). **PROPOSED** = from the author's roadmap discussion or this draft. **TO LOCK** = needs the author's yes before any checklist or chapter is built. Where the author's earlier designs (the BG2/IWD-style purification battle, the holy-water campaign, Xima's mind-risk, the arc list XXIV-XXXV) are not in my notes, I say so rather than invent them.

## 1. The shape
**15/15 Evils → the restoration network fully awakens → the Black Pearl prophecy activates → Dima and Xima rise (confrontation and purification) → the truth about the compass-star adviser and the ancient force → Jade makes the Gold Child's choice → MAIN QUEST COMPLETE (Curse of the Hidden Isle) → the world stays open.**
Proposed arc map (the author's roadmap; titles and order are as given, details not in my notes):
| Arc | Role |
|---|---|
| XXIII The Winter That Would Not End | Endless Winter restored: **8 / 15** |
| XXIV The Lives We Choose | relationship arc, no Evil, no Pearl |

XXV to XXXI is seven arcs, so it fits exactly if XXV and XXVII-XXIX are one Evil each (which of the other four goes where is TO LOCK). XXXII-XXXV carry no Evil, so the tracker reads 15 / 15 from XXXI on.

## 3. Decisions to lock
**D1. Who is the pearl's "true owner"?** *The mural (53): "When the pearl returns to its true owner, the two sisters shall rise again."* **Recommendation: the owner is the restoration network itself, not a person.** Ch184 already makes Devon's family custodians who carried it "until the restoration network was needed again" (a trust, not a possession). Devon is the rightful bearer; at the right moment he **willingly returns it to the system it anchors**. That keeps Devon central, makes "return" an act of choice, and explains the prophecy line. *Game consequence to decide:* does Devon keep his Pearl skills? Recommendation: yes; Black Pearl Resonance becomes the **Dragon Manifestation** state (the parked evolution), because he is now the network's bearer, not the pearl's owner. Status: ☐ TO LOCK.
**D2. What does "the two sisters shall rise again" mean?** **Recommendation: Dima and Xima, both.** Ch103 says Dima "has regained her power"; treat that as partial (power, not her true state). The False Ancestor (335) explicitly "is not Xima", so Xima is unresolved. The Pearl-and-network event restores both to the state the prophecy means. *New interpretation, needs light wording care around ch103.* *The author must define "true state"* (e.g. embodied rather than spirit-voice). Status: ☐ TO LOCK.
**D3. The responsibility hierarchy** (protects against a good-sister/evil-sister ending): Xima did terrible things; Dima also made consequential choices; their conflict was exploited; the ancient force benefited from a divided world. Jade learns "the history was more complicated than either side understood", not "Xima was innocent". Matches ch142-146 (they began as allies; "our conflict was real, but also used"). Status: ☐ TO LOCK (recommended).
**D4. "Through love, the worlds may be united or destroyed".** **Recommendation: the worlds are the sealed regions and the world's divided structure**, not another romantic choice. Two scales: the personal one is paid (Jade chose Devon, 59/103); the world one is Jade's final choice. *The source does not say this outright: keep it marked as our interpretation.* The **Passages tab** already exists for this (region statuses Open, Conditional, Shared, Restricted, Sealed, Unclassified, set only by the story) and already lists **Dima's Sanctuary** and **Xima Realm** with no rules: natural hooks. Status: ☐ TO LOCK.
**D5. The ancient force and the compass-star adviser.** They must deepen the myth without replacing Xima as the villain. **Recommendation: reveal them in XXXV, after the sisters' climax.** Open: what the force is, whether the adviser is one being or "a pattern" (ch188: "the name is a pattern, not a single being"; Register anomalies beyond the Fifteen) and whether it counts as a sixteenth entity. Status: ☐ TO LOCK (needs the author's design).
**D6. The Gold Child's choice (the real finale).** Not "can Jade kill Xima", but "after the sisters are dealt with, what happens to the world they fractured?" Dima (146): "The Gold bloodline was never meant to defeat darkness. It was meant to choose what comes after." Jade's role is judgment (fits her Imperial Guard identity). **Recommendation: not a binary.** Options to lock: (a) reunite everything, (b) keep the seals, (c) region by region, staged, using the Passages statuses, with the force's threat addressed. Whatever is chosen sets the postgame world state. Status: ☐ TO LOCK (the author's call).
**D7. Roles of the original trio.** Jade: the chooser, the bridge. Devon: the Pearl custodian, the restoration anchor. Sky: the Returning Light, a healing-and-restoration conduit in the network mechanics. Not "Sky is secretly more important"; three different functions. Ch610's line ("one day that habit may save someone very important to you") stays foreshadowing. **The Sky and Jenika cure and pregnancy storyline stays separate**, as already decided. Status: ☐ TO LOCK.
**D8. The Pearl Chamber and Dragon Manifestation come back.** In the code the Dragon Sanctuary and **Pearl Chamber are sealed placeholders (ch999)** and the boss `boss_pearl_guardian` is a level-18 fight (860 HP): far too small, to be rebuilt as an endgame fight. Bring them into XXXII, not before. Status: ☐ TO LOCK.
**D9. The Dima and Xima battle and the preparation campaign (XXXIII-XXXV).** The author's earlier designs (the BG2/IWD-style purification battle, the holy-water campaign, strengthening Dima, weakening Xima, allies, Xima's mind-risk) **are not in my notes: send them** and I will fold them in here. Status: ☐ NEEDS THE AUTHOR'S DESIGN.
**D10. Ending state and postgame.** "MAIN QUEST COMPLETE: Curse of the Hidden Isle" banner, then the world stays open. To lock: what the banner sets (a flag such as `main_quest_complete`), which story threads continue after it (the Jenika storyline, Elise's departure as narration, Aster and Fen as true love), and which systems stay live (Questions tab, desks, Expedition). Status: ☐ TO LOCK.

He keeps his skills and Dragon Manifestation; he does not lose anything mechanically by returning the stone.

Before XXXII: Dima has restored consciousness and power (ch103) but an incomplete manifestation; Xima is fragmented, remote influence only (the False Ancestor was explicitly not Xima). After the Pearl and network activation both can manifest as themselves. This does not contradict ch103.
- **D3 LOCKED as written.** Xima remains responsible; Dima also made consequential choices; their conflict was exploited. Never "Xima was innocent".
- **D4 LOCKED as our interpretation (mark it so).** "The worlds" = the sealed regions and the world's divided structure. The personal love prophecy has paid off (Jade chose Devon freely). The early romantic reading was not wrong; the endgame adds the larger meaning: connection cannot be forced because a prophecy says so.
- **D5 LOCKED placement only.** Revealed in XXXV after the sisters' climax, not replacing it. The ancient force and the adviser are **outside the Fifteen** (no "15/16"). Locked: the adviser is connected to the ancient force and deliberately manipulated the Dima/Xima conflict. **Deliberately undecided** until XXXV: avatar / servant / identity repeated across generations / an office or symbol carried by several people.
- **D6 LOCKED.** Staged, region-by-region restoration, not a binary. Jade decides which passages can safely open, which need conditions, which stay sealed, which become shared routes. Maps onto the Passages statuses (Open, Conditional, Shared, Restricted, Sealed, Unclassified); the final states are set by her main-story decisions, with a canonical default allowed.
- **D7 LOCKED.** Jade = chooser; Devon = Pearl and restoration anchor; Sky = Returning Light and healing conduit. Ch610's line stays about Jenika; Sky's network role is an additional payoff, not a retroactive replacement.
- **D8 LOCKED for XXXII.** Rebuild the sealed Pearl Chamber and Dragon Sanctuary placeholders (ch999, boss_pearl_guardian lvl 18 / 860 HP) as real endgame content, and bring back Dragon Manifestation.
