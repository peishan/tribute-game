# MASTER YALE: allied quest giver and specialist healer (design locked by the author; provisional until Arc XXIX is drawn)

**Role:** a recurring allied quest giver and specialist, **stationary at Lingering Vale**, never a permanent member of the five-person travelling party. He raised Sky and trained him as a healer, knows the Vale and keeps his own healing tradition: that setup earns a real gameplay role, not just a paternity-reveal NPC.

- **Location:** Lingering Vale (his own hub; not built yet, only the Road Southwest to it exists).
- **Title:** Master Healer / Senior Quest Giver.
- **Specialty:** medicine, spiritual diagnosis, curse treatment, rare herbs, difficult recovery cases.
- **Unlock:** when Arc XXIX reaches the Vale; more services once the **Weeping Stone** is resolved.
- **Relationship:** an independent ally to Jade's party, with especially strong ties to Sky.

## Quests (to be fitted to what the chapters show)
Medicinal plants; diseased spiritual creatures; patients ordinary healers cannot diagnose; lost healer texts; apprentices; treant-related corruption; later, restoration-network anomalies.

## Yale's Clinic (practical function)
- advanced condition removal;
- rare medicine crafting;
- permanent upgrades to Sky's healer abilities;
- special diagnosis quests;
- Lingering Vale remedies unavailable elsewhere.

## The father reveal is now canon (ch753)
The Blood Kinship Recognition at the Weeping Stone shows **Yale is Sky's father** (father and son, not ambiguous). Sky took Yale's surname by the tradition of disciples, which gave nothing away (ch747). Yvette Sue Valen never told Yale, and why is unanswered (ch755): Yale says he will find her someday. They keep "Master" and "son" together: "you have always been my son in every way that truly matters" (ch754).

## After the father reveal (design note)
Yale does not stop being Sky's master and become "Dad NPC": their old relationship stays intact and some quests change subtly. (Yale now knows (ch753).)

## Where he sits among healers
- **Jenika:** the Pavilion's medical authority in Dragonvale.
- **Master Yale:** the traditional healer and master at Lingering Vale.
- **Sky:** the travelling field healer who learned from Yale and has developed beyond him.

## Built so far
Lingering Vale is a place (ch739), Yale appears in ch740-755 as drawn. The hub services (Clinic, quests) are still not built: the Weeping Stone is now resolved (ch759), so the Clinic and quests can be built when you want them.
