# PROVISIONAL WORLD MAP: checked against the built game and the Wider World map
The author's draft (a vertical diagram: Altan, Dragonvale, Lingering Vale, Frostveil, the Valen Western Territory, Black Forest, Celestial Ruins, Forgotten Road, the Sanctuary of Forgotten Light, Ember Sands, Forgotten Temple, Veilspire Graveyard, the Eastern Sea, the Sunken Kingdom, Tribute) was compared with the game's regions, routes and maps (`LOCATIONS`, `ROUTES`, `WORLD_MAPS`) and with the in-game **Wider World** map (`assets/maps/wider_world.webp`, sent by the author at ch310). Distances and orientation are flexible, as the draft says; these are the places where it contradicts what is built or the author's own map.

## What the author's Wider World map shows (the reference)
North: **Altan Kingdom** (north-west), **Dragonvale** (north, beside Altan) and **Tribute** (north-east); the **Valen Borderlands** in the middle; the **Western Waters** to the west (Frontier Port, Western Archive, Road of Foreign Stars, Old Trade Sea, the Sanctuary of Open Sky); a large red wasteland to the south, **The Fifteen Evils: Cursed Lands**; the **Sunken Kingdom** in the south-centre beside it; **Broken Seals** (ancient ruins) east of Tribute; **Impossible Entry** (forbidden region) to the south-east; **Dima's Sanctuary** and **Xima's Realm** (locked) far south-east. Tribute is **not** at the bottom.

## Matches
- Altan is a separate kingdom beside Dragonvale and Tribute, in the north (Altan Route, 5 days by sea from Tribute; 6 by sea from Dragonvale; the Falcon Road inside Altan).
- Dragonvale is the royal and family hub (Aster and Fen, Roc and Delilah, Jenika's Pavilion).
- The Valen Borderlands are the western land (Tribute to the Valen Borderlands by the Western Road, 4 days).
- Frostveil is a region of the Dragonvale side (4 days north of Dragonvale in the game); the Endless Winter is restored there.
- The Sunken Kingdom is an ancient underwater civilisation tied to the Drowned Crown, the Black Pearl and the restoration network.
- Tribute: the Imperial Palace, Greyson, the Imperial Guard base, Adrian and Evelyne, the mission hub.

## Conflicts with what is built
1. **Tribute's position.** The draft puts Tribute at the bottom across the Eastern Sea. On the Wider World map and in the routes it sits beside Dragonvale (Tribute to Dragonvale: 6 days by road or 4 by the Eastern Sea Passage; also upriver from Faepool Harbour via River Crossing). The Altan Route sails from Tribute directly.
2. **The Sunken Kingdom's position.** Built, it is reached **from Dragonvale** (the Forbidden Sea Route, 3 days, then the Submerged Passage). The Wider World map puts it in the south-centre. The draft puts it between the Eastern Sea and Tribute; a link to Tribute is plausible (the Pearl, the sunken records) but not a built route.
3. **Frostveil.** The draft draws it below Dragonvale with Lingering Vale. In the game it is north of Dragonvale. (The Silent Mountains are 5 days north; the Bone River is a placeholder I placed 5 days east of Dragonvale: the pages name no place.)
4. **The Black Forest** is in the **north** (Tribute to the Northern Frontier, then Black Forest, Forest of Thorns, Mourning Valley, Crownless Marches; Arc VII), not in the west.
5. **The Celestial Ruins, the Forgotten Road and the Sanctuary of Forgotten Light are all in the Valen Borderlands chain, in the opposite order to the draft.** Built: Valen Borderlands, Forgotten Battlefield, **Forgotten Sanctuary** (the Sanctuary of Forgotten Light), then **the Forgotten Road (3 days)**, then **Celestial Ruins**, then the Opened Passage to the **Land Beyond the Seal**. The draft puts the Celestial Ruins as an "ancient northern zone" and the Sanctuary below the Forgotten Road.
6. **Lingering Vale (settled by the author): Sky's childhood home and Roc's cover.** Ch15: Chad (Roc) says he is from Lingering Vale; it is also the Vale node on the Dragonvale map (south-west of the capital, drakes and old guardians). Both fit: it stays a vale at Dragonvale's edge, which Roc used as his cover and where Sky grew up with Master Yale (the Weeping Stone arc, XXIX). It is not a separate continental region.
7. **The "Forgotten Temple"** belongs on the Dragonvale side, not the south-west. **Correction (fourth draft check below): it is the Moonveil Temple** (ch78 "Echoes of the Forgotten Temple": the hidden mountain sanctuary), a separate place, not part of the Dragon Pearl Sanctuary.

## In the draft, not in the game yet
**Ember Sands**, the **Veilspire Graveyard**, **Seris Valen** and **Lingering Vale as a region** are new (no mention in the scripts). The Wider World map's red southern wasteland (The Fifteen Evils: Cursed Lands) is a natural home for the Ember Sands and the Veilspire Graveyard (old Fifteen-Evil and sealing history). The Weeping Stone (Arc XXIX) has no place yet.

**Two corrections and one provisional route.**

The **Moonveil Temple / Forgotten Temple is separate**, 1 day east of the capital (the Eastern Mountain Path).
3. **Bone River: 5 days east of Dragonvale, provisional** (placement pending explicit geographic naming). Chapters 661-666 are built around that route; if a later chapter names the region differently the place can be repositioned without changing the travel event.
**Geography versus jurisdiction (decision):** being north of Dragonvale does not make a place Dragonvale sovereign territory. Map: the Silent Mountains and Frostveil are branches of the Dragonvale travel network. Code: the territory tags are **left unchanged** (Frostveil, the Silent chain and the Bone River keep the placeholder `tribute` tag) until sovereignty is decided explicitly, and **Roc's "Dragonvale ground" guest rule is not expanded** to them. The only retag made is the Northern Frontier of Arc XX, which the author confirmed is Dragonvale's own.
